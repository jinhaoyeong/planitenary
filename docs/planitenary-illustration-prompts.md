# Planitenary Illustration Prompts

Production-ready prompts for generating the main editorial illustrations used in the approved Planitenary redesign concepts.

These prompts produce illustration assets only. They intentionally exclude interface controls, text, logos, and device frames so the artwork can be cropped and positioned responsively inside the application.

## Shared visual language

All five illustrations should share these characteristics:

- Editorial travel illustration
- Flat gouache-like colour fields
- Japanese print and mid-century travel-poster influence
- Slightly elevated or bird's-eye viewpoint
- Tiny travellers or vehicles within large landscapes
- Marigold yellow, forest green, muted river blue, cream, and charcoal
- Fine graphite linework
- Dry-brush edges and tactile paper grain
- Simplified geometry with imperfect printed registration
- Calm, spacious composition
- No photorealism, glossy 3D, gradients, or slick corporate-vector styling

## 1. My Trips — Golden road and green bus

Corresponding reference: `exec-f5489c10-91af-456f-8d6f-d4d3959f2cab.png`

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: responsive trip-card hero illustration for a travel-planning application

Primary request:
Create an original editorial travel illustration of a small green minibus travelling alone along a winding pale country road through enormous golden-yellow fields.

Scene:
A high, slightly isometric bird's-eye view. The pale road enters from the lower-left area, curves gently through the centre, and disappears near the upper-right edge. Dense golden fields occupy most of the image. Irregular dark forest-green hedges follow parts of the road. Place one substantial dark-green tree near a lower corner.

Subject:
A tiny boxy green minibus with cream roof details, positioned close to the visual centre. It should feel like a traveller beginning a long journey rather than a commercial vehicle.

Style:
Flat editorial travel illustration combining gouache, screen printing, risograph texture, and restrained Japanese print influence. Broad simplified shapes, dry-brush boundaries, fine charcoal details, subtle imperfect ink registration, and visible paper grain. Evocative and handcrafted, not photorealistic.

Composition:
Wide landscape composition with strong negative space. Keep the bus and the main road inside a centre-safe region so the artwork can also be recomposed for a portrait mobile crop. The winding road must remain the dominant visual gesture.

Color palette:
Saturated marigold yellow, deep forest green, warm cream, charcoal grey, and a small amount of muted olive.

Lighting and mood:
Warm, quiet daytime; optimistic, contemplative, and unhurried.

Constraints:
Illustration only. No interface, cards, buttons, text, typography, logos, road signs, watermark, border, or device frame. No photographic rendering, glossy 3D, gradients, neon colours, or generic corporate-vector style.
```

Recommended master size: `1536 × 1024` landscape.

## 3. Plan a Trip — Train through yellow fields

Corresponding reference: `exec-02fb8b66-f029-43c6-be44-7634271f860a.png`

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: trip-setup screen illustration for a travel-planning application

Primary request:
Create an original editorial travel illustration of a small white regional train curving through dense golden-yellow flower fields beside a broad blue lake.

Scene:
An elevated scenic viewpoint. The train enters from the lower-left foreground and follows a curving railway toward the middle distance. Golden flowering fields fill the foreground and both sides of the track. Beyond them sits a calm muted-blue lake with one tiny sailboat. Layered blue-grey mountains form the horizon. A small traditional pavilion or pagoda appears far across the water.

Subject:
A modest white-and-deep-green regional train. Through one window, show the tiny simplified silhouette of a seated traveller looking outside. The traveller must remain secondary to the landscape.

Style:
Flat narrative travel illustration with gouache colour blocks, screen-printed grain, fine graphite outlines, dry-brush plant texture, and subtle Japanese editorial-print influence. Handcrafted, calm, and tactile.

Composition:
Portrait-oriented master artwork. The curving railway should lead the eye from the foreground train toward the water and distant pavilion. Reserve pale open sky in the upper section. Keep the train, lake, and pavilion positioned so the scene can later be recomposed into a shallow mobile banner.

Color palette:
Golden marigold, deep green, warm cream, desaturated lake blue, blue-grey mountains, and charcoal linework.

Lighting and mood:
Clear gentle morning; hopeful, organised, and quietly exciting.

Constraints:
Illustration only. No interface, form fields, text, logo, watermark, border, or device frame. No photorealism, anime character styling, shiny 3D train, gradients, or overcrowded scenery.
```

Recommended desktop master size: `1024 × 1536` portrait.

```text
Recompose the same scene as a 3:2 landscape illustration. Preserve the train design, palette, texture, lake, mountains, and pavilion. Use case: stylized-concept
```
```text
Asset type: editorial discovery-card illustration for a travel-planning application

Create an original elevated-view illustration of a solitary traveller walking along a winding cream path beside a blue rocky stream toward a small timber garden pavilion.

A peaceful fictional riverside garden. The stream runs diagonally along the left side with irregular grey stones and hand-drawn ripples. A pale walking path curves through a wide carpet of saturated yellow ground cover. A small traditional timber pavilion with a dark blue-grey tiled roof stands on the right. Saturated golden yellow, deep forest green, muted mineral blue, warm cream, dark blue-grey, and charcoal.

Lighting and mood:
Quiet late morning; contemplative, welcoming, and uncrowded.
```

For the real application, generated artwork like this should introduce discovery or fill an empty state. Actual place cards should continue using authentic, credited photographs.

## 5. Day Plan — Kyoto route from day into night

Corresponding reference: `exec-6e65bbca-116e-4e2d-ad22-415c2ca5489f.png`

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: tall itinerary-day route illustration for a travel-planning application

Primary request:
Create an original vertical editorial illustration that follows one Kyoto travel day from a bright yellow morning landscape into a deep-blue evening canal district.

Scene:
A continuous tall composition divided naturally by the route rather than by panels.

At the top:
Pale sky with delicate hand-drawn clouds, distant blue-grey mountains, a small pagoda, and simplified golden city rooftops.

Through the middle:
A pale winding riverside road and pedestrian path following a muted-blue stream. Include a tiny green tram or local bus and one small walking traveller. Add a flowering tree beside the water.

At the bottom:
Transition gradually into evening. Show dark indigo water, traditional wooden townhouses, a willow tree, one warm paper lantern, and a small cream crescent moon. Reflections should be simplified flat shapes rather than realistic lighting.

Subject:
The winding route is the primary subject. The tiny traveller and transit vehicle provide scale and connect the different parts of the day.

Style:
Vertical narrative travel print with flat gouache colour fields, fine charcoal and graphite linework, screen-print grain, dry-brush edges, subtly imperfect registration, and warm textured paper. Japanese editorial landscape influence without copying any existing artwork.

Composition:
Tall portrait illustration. The route should create a continuous visual path from the upper daylight city through the middle river scene and into the lower nighttime district. Keep the scene readable at narrow sidebar width.

Color palette:
Warm cream, marigold yellow, soft sage, deep forest green, muted river blue, dark indigo, charcoal, and tiny warm lantern accents.

Lighting and mood:
A calm progression from fresh morning to intimate evening. Organised, walkable, and human in scale.

Constraints:
Illustration only. No timeline UI, itinerary text, labels, map pins, buttons, logo, watermark, frame, or device. No photographic realism, neon cyberpunk lighting, gradients, anime styling, glossy 3D, or excessive architectural detail.
```

Recommended master size: `1024 × 1536` portrait.

## Consistency follow-up prompt

After approving the first generated asset, attach it to each subsequent request and append:

```text
Use the attached approved illustration as the visual-system anchor. Match its exact paper colour, ink density, grain scale, edge roughness, line weight, vehicle simplification, and yellow-green-blue colour relationships. Create a new composition, but make it unmistakably part of the same illustration family. Do not introduce a new rendering style.
```

## Production note

Generate separate desktop and mobile compositions when their aspect ratios differ significantly. Avoid relying on automatic centre crops when they would remove the traveller, vehicle, route, pavilion, or other narrative focal point.
